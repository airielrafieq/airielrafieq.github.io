Project 01 • IoT & Safety

IoSafe — Student Safety IoT System

A student safety concept designed to help students in college and hostel environments send emergency alerts to wardens and selected contacts.

CourseCT115 / CT125
PlatformESP32 · Firebase
RoleTeam Leader
01 / Details

Project Overview

The Problem

Emergency Delays

Students living in hostels or colleges may not be able to call for help quickly during an emergency such as a fall, an accident or a safety threat.

The Solution

IoT Incident Alerting

IoSafe uses an ESP32 microcontroller and sensors to support manual SOS activation and fall or impact detection alongside real-time location tracking for wardens.
